Deeper analytical views built on the same real ACLED (Armed Conflict Location & Event Data) export as the Overview page, 1 January 2022 to 7 August 2026: 351,530 events, 275,662 fatalities across 2 countries/territories. Lethality

Lethality rate: fatalities per recorded event, ranked by country

Total fatalities ÷ total events per country, for the currently selected date range and event type, ranked highest to lowest. A time-series view per country (one line each) was not used here, since 15 overlapping lines are unreadable at this scale: this ranked view keeps every country comparable at a glance. Click a bar to filter the rest of the page to that country.

Countries with zero events in the current filter are omitted (an undefined rate, not a zero one).

Trends by country

Country trend comparison (small multiples)

Every configured country on its own scale, so lower-volume countries aren't flattened by a much larger country's totals on a shared axis. Switch the metric above; granularity, date range and event-type filters (in the toolbar) apply to every panel. Click a panel to filter the rest of the page to that country.
Year-over-year

Year-over-year period comparison

Each selected year plotted across the full calendar year (Jan–Dec), aligned by day-of-year so the same week lines up across years. The shaded band marks the highlighted period set below, applied identically to every year shown. Region and event-type filters (in the toolbar above) apply; the date-range filter doesn't, since this chart always compares full calendar years.

Highlighted-period totals are summed from daily records against the picked calendar dates (day-of-year), so the same window is compared across every year regardless of which year the pickers above happen to show. The current year's line stops where the data does, rather than dropping to zero for months not yet reached.
